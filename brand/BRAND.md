## Brand Mark Description

Four gold figures arranged around a central cross on a deep black ground.
- Two adult figures above, two children below
- All four connected by sweeping arcs forming an enclosing circle
- Central cross glowing with warm light
- Gold treatment: rich, warm, not yellow — communicates identity, significance, achievement
- Deep black base: cinematic, premium, not grey

## Family Ethos

**Christ at the Centre. Excellence in All.**

This is an optional contextual line. It is not automatically appended to every logo appearance.

## Creative Concept — Launch Ident

**"Four Become One"** is the creative concept behind the planned launch ident.
It is NOT the permanent Family OS tagline.

Ident sequence (approved direction, not yet implemented):
```
four independent points
→ movement / convergence
→ family form resolves
→ central cross revealed
→ Olatoye Family OS mark
→ transition into Home
```

Duration: approximately 2–4 seconds.
Must be original — not an imitation of Netflix, Disney, Apple, Sky or any other brand.

## Visual Direction

| Attribute    | Value                                                                 |
|--------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Mood         | Premium, cinematic, warm, modern, family-centred, restrained          |
| Canvas       | Deep near-black cinematic base                                        |
| Primary accent | Warm gold — identity, focus, significance, achievement              |
| Gold usage   | Restrained — not sprayed across every UI element                      |
| TV target    | 10-foot premium television experience                                 |
| Artwork led  | Hero imagery, content artwork drive visual weight — not chrome        |

## What Gold Communicates

Gold is used for:
- Brand identity marks
- Focus/selection states
- Achievement and significance
- Navigation active state

Gold is NOT used for:
- Generic decorative elements
- Every button or label
- Anything that should recede visually
