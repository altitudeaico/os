Our Digital Rulebook

We want Elsie and Emma to be really good with technology — not afraid of it. Our job as parents is to give them more freedom as they become ready for it, while carrying the risks they are still too young to carry themselves.

The simple idea

Freedom within boundaries. The girls should be able to create, learn and explore. Mum and Dad put sensible boundaries around things such as strangers, privacy, public posting, unsuitable content and account security. As their judgement grows, so does their freedom.

What this means in our family
