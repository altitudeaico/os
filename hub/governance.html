Management view

Each line answers four questions: what v0.1 said, what we have observed or built, what it now means in practice, and whether the next framework should keep, add or refine it.

1. Core philosophy
v0.1: freedom within boundaries; capability earns autonomy.
The build has reinforced rather than challenged the original philosophy.
Keep as-is
2. Capabilities
v0.1: Create · Publish · Consume · Interact.
Guardian has made granular capability governance real. Govern what a child can do, not simply whether an app is allowed.
Strengthen
3. Context
v0.1: not explicitly defined.
The same activity can be appropriate on a family TV but not on an unsupervised personal device. Permission needs context.
Add
4. Operating modes
v0.1: not defined.
Academy, leisure, bedtime and family viewing can legitimately operate under different rules.
Add
5. Progressive autonomy
v0.1: Guided → Governed → Trusted → Independent.
A child may be Trusted at creating while still Governed at publishing or interacting. Progress should be capability-specific.
Refine
6. Guardian
v0.1: safeguarding/policy layer that enforces boundaries.
Guardian should decide and record policy; Family OS, Qustodio, Apple, Amazon and others may perform enforcement.
Clarify
7. Algorithms & discovery
v0.1: consumption should be curated or filtered.
Choosing a video is different from entering an unrestricted algorithmic feed. The Firm should increasingly control the front door to discovery.
Strengthen
8. Devices & Surfaces
v0.1: govern devices and internet.
TVs and shared displays are Surfaces, not people. Each Surface needs its own identity, access and information boundary.
Add
9. Parent control
v0.1: The Firm owns governance and immature risk.
Central Control gives Mum and Dad one practical place to see and administer that authority.
Operationalise
10. Child visibility
v0.1: largely parent/Firm focused.
My Digital World should let the girls understand what they can do, what needs approval and how autonomy grows.
Add
11. Monitoring & privacy
v0.1: protection is not surveillance.
Guardian should collect the minimum information necessary for protection, not everything technology makes observable.
Strengthen
12. Creator presence
v0.1: child learns creator workflow; The Firm retains external risk.
Real creative activity validates the model: child owns creative expression; The Firm controls the external perimeter.
Promote to operating policy
13. Public exposure
v0.1: identity risk is cumulative.
Assess what information reveals in combination, especially identity, routines and location, rather than relying only on blanket bans.
Strengthen
14. Security posture
v0.1: defence in depth.
Guardian's build adds a useful rule: uncertainty must never silently create additional privilege or access.
Add
15. Mistakes & trust
v0.1: autonomy grows with demonstrated judgement.
Safe disclosure matters: telling Mum or Dad about a mistake, suspicious contact or uncomfortable event should count as responsible behaviour.
Add
16. Framework vs daily rules
v0.1: principles and future decisions sit together.
Apps, permissions and devices change too often to live in the constitution. Separate durable governance from the living rulebook.
Structural change

Proposed governance stack

1 · Digital Governance FrameworkThe durable constitution — principles, authority, risk, autonomy and governance model.
↓
2 · Family Digital Policy RegisterThe living rulebook — current permissions, services, devices, modes and approval requirements for Elsie and Emma.
↓
3 · GuardianThe machine-readable policy and enforcement coordination layer.
